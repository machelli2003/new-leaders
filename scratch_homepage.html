Welcome to NEW LEADERS HOTEL AND EVENTS CENTRE

Our Accommodations

 To meet the demands of our visitors, we provide a range of lodging alternatives. We have the ideal room for both solo and group travel. Our rooms provide cozy mattresses, contemporary conveniences, and breathtaking city views.  

Our Dining

  At NEW LEADERS HOTEL AND EVENTS CENTRE, we take pride in offering a diverse range of dining options. From our on-site restaurant serving local cuisine to our rooftop bar and lounge with breathtaking views of the city, we have something for every palate. Our expert chefs use only the freshest ingredients to create delicious meals that will leave you craving more.

Our Location

  Located in the diaspora of the city, NEW LEADERS HOTEL AND EVENTS CENTRE is the perfect base for exploring the sights and sounds of the city. We are within a 5-minute drive from Akyem Tafo township to Tontro road. Our prime location offers convenient access to public transportation, making it easy to explore the city.
